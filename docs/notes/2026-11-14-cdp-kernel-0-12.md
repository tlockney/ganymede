## 2026-11-14, pin cdp-kernel 0.12.0 (#792)

cdp-kernel 0.12.0 adds two things:

- Paging through the kernel: a table cut by the cap is kept in the
  kernel, and the apps ask for more rows over the `ganymede.table.v1`
  comm (cdp-kernel#489).
- The browser kernel's `modules` option: the apps use it to serve
  vega, vega-lite and linkedom themselves, so charts draw offline
  (cdp-kernel#491).

CI's e2e suite, the container image and the docs that name the tested
version move to 0.12.0. docs/data-output.md now describes both, with
"Unreleased in ganymede-ui" notes. The app side ships in
ganymede-ui's next release: paging in ganymede-ui#605 (issue #604), and
offline charts in ganymede-ui#611 (issue #610), both for #747.
