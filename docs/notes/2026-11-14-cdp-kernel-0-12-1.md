## 2026-11-14, cdp-kernel 0.12.1 (#799)

cdp-kernel 0.12.1 adds `SQL_MODULES` to the browser kernel
(cdp-kernel#495). A page that maps DuckDB's module, wasm and worker in
`modules` runs `jupyter.sql` without jsDelivr. ganymede-ui#615 (issue
ganymede-ui#614) uses it: it bundles the module and fetches the wasm
and worker into the page's cache the first time it is online, a choice
Thomas made over shipping 36 MB in the apps.

Here the pins move to 0.12.1 (CI, the container image, the
architecture and development docs), and docs/data-output.md describes
offline SQL, with its "Unreleased in ganymede-ui" note widened to cover
it.
